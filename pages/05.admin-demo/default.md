# Look inside the builder

Every form on this site is open in **Admin Next**, Grav 2's admin. Sign in with the demo account and look around. It is read-only: you can open, drag, edit and undo as much as you like, but saving is refused and nothing on the site changes.

- **Sign in at:** [/admin](/admin)
- **Username:** `demo`
- **Password:** `FormsDemo2026`

## What to try

- Open **Forms Pro** in the sidebar. The **Forms** list has every form from these demos.
- Open **Join Lumen & Pine** and click a step's tab, then a field, to see its settings in the panel on the right.
- Open the **Logic** tab of **Request a project quote** to see the rules written out as sentences.
- Open **Website cost estimator** and click a calculation. The formula editor lists the fields and functions, and its **Try it** box works out results for sample values.
- The **Entries** tabs of **Contact the studio**, **Book a workshop** and **How did your project go?** have sample submissions to search, filter and open.
- Press **Save** or `Cmd+S` after a change and see the refusal.

Forms Pro is a premium plugin for Grav 2, at **$75**. [Read about Forms Pro](https://getgrav.org/premium/forms-pro) or [browse the documentation](https://learn.getgrav.org/2/grav-premium/forms-pro).
