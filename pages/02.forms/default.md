# Form demos

Six forms that submit, each showing one part of Forms Pro. On this demo site every form only shows a thank-you message when it is sent: there are no emails, no webhooks and no stored entries, and uploaded files go to a temporary folder that is emptied every hour.

- [**Contact with recipient routing**](/forms/contact): the visitor chooses who reads their message.
- [**Multi-step job application**](/forms/job-application): four steps with numbered circles and a required file upload.
- [**Quote request with conditional logic**](/forms/project-quote): questions that come and go with each answer.
- [**Workshop booking with a repeater**](/forms/workshop-booking): a progress bar, a live total and a row per attendee.
- [**Feedback survey with tabs**](/forms/feedback-survey): the tabs progress style with a rating and a slider.
- [**Spam protection**](/forms/spam-protection): a honeypot, a math captcha and Cap proof of work.

The [pro fields](/pro-fields) and the [calculator gallery](/calculators) have pages of their own.
