## Look inside the builder

Every demo here is open in **Admin Next**. Sign in, open **Forms Pro** in the sidebar and click through the builder, the **Logic** tab, the formula editor and the **Entries** of three forms we filled with sample submissions. It is read-only, so poke at anything you like: saving is refused and nothing changes.

| Sign in at | Username | Password |
|---|---|---|
| [/admin](/admin) | `demo` | `FormsDemo2026` |

Forms Pro is a premium plugin for Grav 2, at **$75**. It needs the free API, Admin Next, Form and Email plugins. [Read about Forms Pro](https://getgrav.org/premium/forms-pro) or [browse the documentation](https://learn.getgrav.org/2/grav-premium/forms-pro).
